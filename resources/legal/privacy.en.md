# My Testimony Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

## 1. Who are we?

My Testimony is a mobile application (Android: `com.askrea.temoignage`, iOS) and a website (https://temoignage.as-krea.com) for sharing and reading testimonies, praying and following events.

It is published by **ARISE AND SHINE KREA** (“we”), [postal address], Benin.

Contact for any question about your data: **[contact e-mail address]**

## 2. In short

- You can use the application **without an account** (guest mode).
- We only collect what is needed for the application to work.
- The testimonies you **publish** are visible to everyone, including visitors without an account and on the website.
- We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.
- You can delete your account and your data at any time, from the application or on request.

## 3. Data we collect

### a) If you use the application without an account (guest mode)

We do not ask you for any personal information. Only the technical data described in point g) is processed.

### b) Account data

Depending on the sign-in method you choose:

- **Google**: your name, your e-mail address, your profile photo and a Google account identifier. We never receive your Google password.
- **E-mail address**: your name, your e-mail address and your password, stored in an encrypted (hashed) form that we cannot read.
- **Phone number**: your number, verified by a code sent by SMS [via Firebase Authentication: to be confirmed].

We also record the account type (person or organization), the application language and, if you add one, your profile photo.

### c) Account verification (“Approved” badge)

If you request verification of your account, we process the information you provide in the verification form. It is used only to decide whether to grant the badge and is not published.

### d) Content you publish

- Testimonies: text, photos, audio recordings and videos, including your conversion testimony.
- Prayer requests you submit.
- Taking part in live broadcasts: if you speak or turn on your camera, your voice and image are broadcast to the participants.

### e) Personal content and data

- Prophetic words you save in order to pray over them.
- Preferences: verse of the day design, font, text size, language.
- Activity in the application: testimonies viewed (for view statistics), prayer time, topics followed.

### f) Notifications

If you allow them, we store a notification identifier (token) specific to your device, in order to send you notifications: video ready, testimony approved, prayer reminders, events…

### g) Technical data

Device model, system and application version, IP address, server error and connection logs. They are used for security and for the proper operation of the service.

[To be confirmed. If the application uses location: “With your permission, the application uses your location for [specific purpose]. You can withdraw this permission at any time in your phone settings.” Otherwise, delete this paragraph and remove the location permission from the application.]

**We do not collect** your contacts, your messages or your banking data.

## 4. Sensitive data: your religious beliefs

A testimony, a prayer request or a prophetic word may reveal your religious beliefs, your health or your personal situation. This information is called “sensitive”.

- We only process it because **you choose** to share it, and with your consent.
- Only publish what you agree to make public, especially when it concerns other people: do not name anyone without their consent.
- You can edit or delete your content at any time.

## 5. Why we use your data

| Purpose | Basis |
|---|---|
| Creating and managing your account, signing you in | Performance of the service |
| Publishing, moderating and displaying testimonies and prayer requests | Your consent and performance of the service |
| Streaming videos and live broadcasts | Performance of the service |
| Sending notifications | Your consent (revocable) |
| View and prayer statistics, featuring testimonies | Our legitimate interest in keeping the community alive |
| Security, abuse prevention, fixing errors | Our legitimate interest and our legal obligations |
| Answering your requests | Your request |

## 6. Who can see your content?

- **Published content** (approved testimonies, conversion testimonies, public prayer requests, public profile: name, photo, badge): visible to all users, to visitors without an account and on the website. It may be shared by other users.
- **Moderation**: our administrators can view submitted content, before or after publication, in order to approve it, reject it or feature it.
- **Personal content** (saved prophetic words, preferences, verification data): visible to you alone and, if necessary, to our administrators.

## 7. Technical service providers

We use service providers who process some data on our behalf, under their own policies:

| Provider | Role | Location |
|---|---|---|
| LWS | Hosting of the website, the API and the database | France |
| Bunny.net (BunnyWay d.o.o.) | Video storage and delivery | European Union, delivered through a global network |
| Google (Google Sign-In, Firebase) | Sign-in with Google [and by phone], push notifications | Worldwide |
| Apple | Notifications on iPhone | Worldwide |
| LiveKit | Live broadcasting | Worldwide |
| YouTube (Google) | Playback of embedded YouTube videos | Worldwide |

These providers may be located outside Benin. We choose them for their security and privacy guarantees.

We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law (request from a competent authority).

## 8. Retention period

- **Account and content**: as long as your account is active.
- **After the account is deleted**: erased within **30 days**, and within **90 days** in backups, unless the law requires us to keep it.
- **Technical logs**: 12 months at most.
- **Inactive accounts**: [3 years] without sign-in, after a warning message.

## 9. Security

Exchanges between the application, the website and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed. Access to the administration tools is limited to authorized people. As no system is infallible, we will inform you of any incident affecting your data, in accordance with the law.

## 10. Your rights

At any time, you can:

- access your data and obtain a copy of it;
- have it corrected;
- have it deleted;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- withdraw your consent, for example by turning off notifications or removing a testimony.

To exercise these rights, write to **[contact e-mail address]** from the address linked to your account, or stating your phone number. We reply within **30 days**.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can contact Benin's **Personal Data Protection Authority (Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel, APDP)**, or the data protection authority of your country.

## 11. Deleting your account

- **In the application**: Settings → Account → **Delete my account**.
- **Without the application**: on the page https://temoignage.as-krea.com/delete-account, or by e-mail to [contact e-mail address].

Deletion erases your profile, your testimonies, your prayer requests, your saved prophetic words, your preferences and your notification tokens, within the periods stated in point 8.

## 12. Children

The application is not intended for children under **[13] years** of age. Between [13] and 18 years of age, a parent or guardian must give their consent before anything is published. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us: we will delete it.

## 13. Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page. In the event of a significant change, we will inform you in the application.
